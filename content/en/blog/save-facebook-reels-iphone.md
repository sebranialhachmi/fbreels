# How to Save Facebook Reels on iPhone (and Where They End Up)

Saving a video on an iPhone feels harder than on Android or a computer, mostly because the file does not appear in your camera roll straight away. The good news is that the process is predictable once you know where iOS puts things. This guide walks through the whole path for a **public** Facebook Reel that you are allowed to keep: copy the link, check it, download the file, find it, and move it to Photos if you want.

## First, a quick reality check

Not every Reel can be saved. A Reel can only be processed when it is public, still available, and when a downloadable file is actually offered for it. Private videos, friends-only posts and clips inside private groups are off limits, and no tool should ask you for your Facebook password to get around that. Also remember that someone else made the video: keep it only if you own it, have permission, or the law clearly allows your use. The [copyright and responsible use guide](blog:copyright-privacy-responsible-use) explains the basics.

## Step 1: Copy the Reel link in the Facebook app

1. Open the Facebook app and find the Reel.
2. Tap the **Share** arrow, usually on the right-hand side under the like and comment buttons.
3. In the share sheet, choose **Copy link**.

Menu labels change between app versions, but a "Copy link" option is nearly always there. If you opened the Reel in a browser instead, copy the full address from the address bar.

Tip: if you tapped **Share** and chose an app like Messages, you will usually get a short share link (for example one containing `/share/r/`). That is fine; the downloader accepts those too.

## Step 2: Open the downloader in Safari

Use **Safari** for this. On iPhone and iPad, Safari has a built-in download manager (since iOS 13), and it saves files in a place you can find. Other browsers may behave differently depending on their version.

Go to the [__BRAND__ homepage](page:home), tap the link box, then tap and hold and choose **Paste**. You can also tap the clipboard button; Safari may ask you to allow pasting, and if it does, tapping **Allow Paste** is fine. Pasting alone sends nothing to our server.

## Step 3: Press Download and read the result

Tap **Download**. The page checks the link and shows what is available. You may see a title and one or more options such as SD or HD video. Only options that genuinely exist are shown. If instead you see a message such as "private or inaccessible" or "no downloadable format", the troubleshooting guide, [why a Facebook Reel download may fail](blog:why-download-fails), explains each one.

## Step 4: Save the file

Tap **Save** beside the option you want. Safari shows a prompt asking whether to download the file. Tap **Download**. A small download icon appears in the Safari toolbar (an arrow in a circle) while the file is coming in.

Download links expire after a short time, around 15 minutes. If you waited too long, simply press Download again on the video to get a fresh one.

## Step 5: Find the file

Tap the download icon in Safari and you will see the file in a list. Tap the magnifying-glass icon beside it to reveal it in the **Files** app. Otherwise open **Files → Browse → Downloads**. Depending on your settings, the Downloads folder is either on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive.

## Step 6 (optional): Put it in Photos

Videos in the Files app are not in your camera roll until you add them:

1. In Files, tap the video to preview it.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Choose **Save Video**.

The video now appears in Photos. If you do not see **Save Video**, the file format is probably not one Photos can play (some web video formats, for example WebM, are not supported). In that case keep it in Files and play it from there, or look for a different quality option when you check the link again.

## Common iPhone problems and fixes

**Nothing happens when I tap Save.** Check that Safari's pop-up and download settings are not blocking it (Settings → Safari). Make sure you have free storage. Try again after reloading the page.

**The file will not play.** Some formats are not supported by Photos. Try the other quality option if one is shown, or open the file with a player app such as VLC.

**The link will not paste.** Tap and hold inside the box to paste manually. If you see "invalid link", copy the complete link again using **Share → Copy link**.

**I cannot find the file.** In Safari tap the download icon, then the magnifier, or search for the file name in the Files app.

**It says the video is private.** Then it cannot be processed. Open the Reel in a private browser window while logged out; if you cannot see it there, it is not public.

## Alternatives built into Facebook

Facebook has its own **Save** feature that bookmarks a Reel or video to your Saved items inside Facebook. This does not put a file on your phone, but it is the easiest way to find a video again later. If you are the creator, you can usually get an original copy of your own videos from your account or Page tools (for example Meta Business Suite or the "Download your information" option in your account settings); the exact menu names change over time.

As a last resort for content you are allowed to keep, the iPhone's built-in **screen recording** can capture a video that is playing. Quality is usually lower than the original file and the recording includes whatever else appears on screen, so it is better to use an original or a proper download when possible.

## A short checklist

1. Copy the link with **Share → Copy link**.
2. Open the downloader in Safari and paste the link.
3. Press **Download** and review the real options.
4. Tap **Save**, then **Download** in Safari's prompt.
5. Find the file in **Files → Downloads**.
6. Use **Share → Save Video** to add it to Photos.
