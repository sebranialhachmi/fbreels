# Copyright and Takedown Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

We respect the rights of creators. __BRAND__ does not host, store or publish videos: it only helps users check public links and, when possible, deliver a file directly to the user's own device. Even so, we take rights-holder concerns seriously.

## What you can do if you own the rights

Because we do not host the videos, the most effective way to remove a video is to remove or restrict it at the source on Facebook, or to report it to Facebook. We can also act on our side, for example by blocking a specific link or content identifier from being processed by this service.

## How to send a request

Use the [contact page](page:contact) and choose **Copyright or takedown request** as the inquiry type. Please include:

1. Your name and a way to reach you.
2. The exact link (URL) of the video you are reporting.
3. Identification of the work you say is infringed, and your relationship to it (owner or authorized representative).
4. A short explanation of why you believe the use is not authorized.
5. A statement that the information you are providing is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights holder.

Incomplete requests may take longer or may not be actionable. Do not send passwords or login details.

## What happens next

We review requests that contain the information above. If a request appears valid, we will take reasonable steps, which may include blocking the reported link from being processed, and we will tell you what we did where we can. We may contact you for clarification. We may decline requests that are incomplete, unclear or that appear abusive or unfounded.

## Responding to a request

If we block a link and the person who submitted it believes this was a mistake, they can write to us through the contact page with the link and an explanation. We will review it in good faith. Because we do not host content, disputes about the underlying video are best resolved between the parties and Facebook.

## Repeat or abusive use

We may restrict access for users who repeatedly attempt to process content in ways that infringe rights or violate our [Terms of Service](page:terms).

## Important notes

This page describes our process. It is not a claim of registration with any copyright office or designated-agent directory, and it is not legal advice. Laws vary by country.
