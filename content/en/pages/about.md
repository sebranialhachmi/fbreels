# About __BRAND__

__BRAND__ is a free web tool that helps people check whether a public Facebook Reel or video can be saved to their device, and then save it when a download option is available.

## Why it exists

People regularly want to keep a recipe video, a family clip they posted themselves, a tutorial, or a Reel they have permission to reuse. Existing tools are often cluttered, confusing, or unclear about what they can actually do. __BRAND__ aims to be a calmer alternative: one text box, clear feedback, and honest explanations when something is not possible.

## Who it is for

- Creators who want a copy of their own public videos.
- People who have permission from the creator to save a clip.
- Anyone who wants to understand why a particular video cannot be downloaded.

## What it does

- Checks a pasted Facebook Reel or video link and shows only the download options that really exist.
- Works on phones, tablets and computers, in English, French, Spanish and Arabic (with right-to-left layout for Arabic).
- Offers a light and a dark theme.
- Explains failures in plain language.

## What it does not do

- It does not download private, friends-only or otherwise protected videos, and it never tries to bypass privacy settings, login requirements or protections.
- It does not ask for your Facebook password, cookies or tokens.
- It cannot guarantee that every public video can be downloaded. Availability depends on the video and on the video service connected to the site.
- It does not host, store or re-publish videos.

## Our principles

1. **Honesty.** We only show results that were actually verified. No invented sizes, qualities or success messages.
2. **Privacy.** We collect as little as practical and explain exactly what we process in the [Privacy Policy](page:privacy).
3. **Respect for creators.** Videos belong to their creators. We provide a [copyright and takedown process](page:copyright) and ask every user to respect it.
4. **Accessibility.** The site is designed for keyboard and screen-reader use. See the [accessibility statement](page:accessibility).
5. **Independence.** __BRAND__ is independent and not affiliated with Facebook or Meta Platforms, Inc. See the [disclaimer](page:disclaimer).
