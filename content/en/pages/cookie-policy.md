# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used on __BRAND__. For how we handle personal data in general, see the [Privacy Policy](page:privacy).

## Essential storage (always on)

These items make the site work the way you expect. They are not used for tracking or advertising.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Cookie | Remembers a language you chose. It is set only when you open the site in a language different from your browser's default (for example by using the language menu). | Up to one year |
| `theme` | Local storage | Remembers whether you chose light or dark theme. | Until you clear it |
| `consent.v1` | Local storage | Remembers your choices about optional cookies. | Until you clear it or change it |

## Optional categories

Optional technologies are **disabled unless the site operator turns them on**, and they never load before you consent (when consent is required). The cookie settings dialog shows only the categories that are actually active on this site; if none is active, the dialog says the site uses only essential storage.

### Analytics (only if enabled)

If the operator enables analytics, __BRAND__ uses Plausible Analytics, which states that it does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It loads only after you allow the Analytics category, and we send only high-level events such as "a download was attempted". We never send the links you submit.

### Advertising (only if enabled)

If the operator enables advertising, __BRAND__ uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set or read cookies and use similar identifiers on your device to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, to personalize them. The specific cookies are controlled by those providers and can change, so we do not list them here. See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires consent to be collected through a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP). The operator can choose either our built-in banner (ads load only after you opt in) or a Google-certified CMP configured in the AdSense account.

## Managing your choices

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer at any time to accept, reject or change optional categories. Rejecting is as easy as accepting. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; this resets your language, theme and consent choices.
