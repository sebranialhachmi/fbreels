# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

This policy explains what information __BRAND__ ("we", "us") processes when you use this website, why, and what choices you have. We try to describe our real behavior precisely. We cannot promise that no data is processed at all, because websites, hosting providers and security systems necessarily process some technical information.

## Who is responsible

The operator of __BRAND__ is the data controller for the processing described here. You can contact the operator through the [contact page](page:contact), choosing "Privacy request".

## What we process

### Links you submit

When you press **Download**, the Facebook link in the box is sent to our server. We validate it, and if a video service is configured, we send the normalized link to that service to find out which download options exist. Nothing is sent when you merely paste or type.

- We keep resolved results in server memory for a few minutes to avoid repeating identical requests, then discard them.
- Our application logs do **not** store the full link or its query string. They record only the host name, a short one-way hash of the path, an outcome code and timing.
- We do not store the videos you download. A file passes through our server only while it is being delivered to you.

### Technical and security data

Like most websites, the servers that host __BRAND__ and any network or security services in front of it may automatically record technical data such as IP address, date and time, requested address, user agent and response status. We use rate limiting to prevent abuse; it uses a one-way hash of your IP address as a key, held in memory (or in a shared store if the operator configured one) for the length of the limit window, which is at most one hour. The hosting provider's own retention periods apply to its logs.

### Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the name, email address, inquiry type and message you provide, in order to respond. The message is delivered by email to the operator through an email delivery service if one is configured. We keep it as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards. Please do not send passwords or Facebook login details.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use a small amount of essential browser storage: your language (a cookie named `NEXT_LOCALE`), your theme (`theme` in local storage) and your cookie choice (`consent.v1` in local storage). Details are in the [Cookie Policy](page:cookies). This storage does not track you across websites.

## Analytics and advertising

This site is built so that analytics and advertising are **off unless the operator enables them**. The cookie settings dialog lists only the categories that are active on the site you are visiting.

- **Analytics.** If enabled, we use Plausible Analytics, which describes itself as cookie-free and privacy-focused, to count visits in aggregate. It loads only after you allow the Analytics category. We do not send submitted links to analytics.
- **Advertising.** If enabled, we show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and, depending on your choices and location, to personalize them. Ad scripts load only after you allow the Advertising category (or, where the operator uses a Google-certified consent management platform, according to that platform's choices). You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

You can accept, reject or change your choice at any time using **Cookie settings** in the footer. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

## Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on: our legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving the service (processing submitted links and technical logs); your request (contact messages); and your consent (optional analytics and advertising technologies).

## Sharing and processors

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as needed: hosting and network providers, the video service used to resolve links (if configured), an email delivery service for the contact form (if configured), and the analytics and advertising providers described above (if enabled). Some of these providers may process data outside your country.

## Retention and deletion

- Resolved link results: a few minutes, in memory.
- Rate-limit keys: at most one hour.
- Application logs: kept only as long as needed for security and debugging, without full links.
- Contact messages: as described above.
- Browser storage: stays on your device until you clear it.

To ask us to delete data we hold about you, contact us through the [contact page](page:contact).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Residents of some regions, including some US states, may have additional rights such as opting out of certain advertising uses. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

## Children

This service is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Security

We use HTTPS, security headers, encrypted short-lived download references and abuse controls. No system is perfectly secure.
