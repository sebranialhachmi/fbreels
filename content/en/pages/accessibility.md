# Accessibility Statement

*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

We want __BRAND__ to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, magnification, or other assistive technology.

## Our goal

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, where practical.

## What we have done

- Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and landmarks, and a "Skip to main content" link.
- All form fields have visible labels, and errors are announced to assistive technology and linked to the field they relate to.
- Everything can be used with a keyboard, with clearly visible focus indicators, including the language menu, theme toggle, mobile menu, FAQ accordion, cookie preferences and the contact form.
- Status messages (for example "Checking the link") are announced by screen readers.
- Animations are subtle and are reduced or removed when your device is set to reduce motion.
- Light and dark themes with text and controls designed for sufficient contrast.
- Correct page language and text direction, including full right-to-left support for Arabic.

## Known limitations

- We have not yet had an independent accessibility audit. Our checks so far are our own automated and keyboard testing.
- Thumbnails and titles shown in results come from the video itself and may not have meaningful descriptions beyond what the video provides.
- Third-party advertising content, when enabled, is outside our control.

## Tell us about a barrier

If you find something that is hard to use, please tell us through the [contact page](page:contact), describing the page, your device and the assistive technology you use. We will do our best to fix it, or to give you the information another way.
