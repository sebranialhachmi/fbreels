# Editorial Policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026.*

The guides and articles on __BRAND__ exist to help people use the tool, understand why downloads sometimes fail, and use videos responsibly. This page explains how we aim to keep them useful and accurate.

## How articles are created

- Topics come from real questions people have: how to save a Reel on a phone, why a download fails, how quality works, what is allowed.
- Articles are **drafted with the help of AI writing tools** and must be read, checked and edited by the site owner before they go live. We do not publish unreviewed AI text.
- Each language version is written for its readers, not translated word for word. Terminology, device steps and examples are adapted.

## What we check

- Instructions must match how the tool actually behaves. We do not describe features that do not exist.
- We do not publish invented statistics, fake studies, made-up quotations, or credentials we do not have. Where we are unsure, we say so.
- Legal topics are explained in general terms and clearly marked as not legal advice.
- Steps for third-party apps (Facebook, iOS, Android) can change with app updates. We describe them in general terms and tell you when menus may look different.

## Dates and updates

Each article shows its publication date. We show a "last reviewed" date only when we have actually re-checked the article. When the tool or a platform changes in a way that affects a guide, we update the guide.

## Corrections

If you spot a mistake or an out-of-date step, tell us through the [contact page](page:contact). We correct confirmed errors promptly and update the article.

## Independence and advertising

Editorial content is not written to promote advertisers. If advertising appears on the site, it is clearly labelled and kept separate from the tool and from the text. See the [disclaimer](page:disclaimer) for our independence from Facebook and Meta.
