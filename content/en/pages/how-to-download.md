# How to download Facebook Reels and videos

This guide shows how to use __BRAND__ to check a public Facebook Reel or video and save it when a download option is available. It takes less than a minute once you know the steps. If you only need the short version, the [homepage](page:home) has a three-step summary right next to the tool.

## Before you start

- Use a **public** Reel or video. Anything private, friends-only, or inside a private group cannot be processed.
- Only save content you own or have permission to keep. See [copyright and responsible use](blog:copyright-privacy-responsible-use).
- You never need to log in to Facebook on this site, and you should never type your Facebook password anywhere on it.

## Step 1: Copy the link

**In the Facebook app (Android, iPhone, iPad):** open the Reel or video, tap the **Share** arrow, then choose **Copy link**.

**On a computer or in a mobile browser:** open the video and copy the full address from the browser's address bar. Links that start with `facebook.com`, `m.facebook.com` or `fb.watch` are supported.

## Step 2: Paste the link and press Download

Open [the downloader](page:home), tap the link box and paste the link. You can also press the clipboard button next to the box; your browser may ask for permission first, and if it refuses you can paste manually with Ctrl+V (or long-press on a phone).

Pasting never sends anything to our server. The link is checked only when you press **Download**.

## Step 3: Review the options

If the video can be processed, you will see its title when available, and a list of the formats that actually exist for it. Typical entries are an SD or HD video file. An audio entry appears only when it is genuinely available. If a quality is not listed, it is not available for that video.

## Step 4: Save the file

Press **Save** next to the option you want. Your browser downloads the file. The download links are short-lived (around 15 minutes); if one expires, press Download again to get a fresh link.

## Where the file goes

- **Android:** Chrome shows a download notification. Open the **Files** app and go to **Downloads**. It may take a moment to appear in your gallery.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Safari asks you to confirm the download. Find the file in **Files → Downloads**. To keep it in Photos, open the file, tap **Share**, then **Save Video**. Our [iPhone guide](blog:save-facebook-reels-iphone) walks through this in detail.
- **Windows, Mac and Linux:** the file goes to your browser's default Downloads folder.

## If it does not work

Most failures have simple causes. The [troubleshooting article](blog:why-download-fails) explains each message, and the [FAQ](page:faq) answers common questions. In short:

1. Copy the link again with **Share → Copy link**.
2. Confirm the video is public by opening it in a private browser window while logged out.
3. Wait a minute and try again if the service is busy.
4. If the video is simply not offered in a downloadable format, there is nothing the tool can do for that video.

## A note on quality

Quality depends on what Facebook provides for each video. The tool does not upscale or enhance videos, and it never promises HD. To understand why some videos have more options than others, read [how to get HD versions when they exist](blog:download-facebook-videos-hd).
